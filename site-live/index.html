Inside Relayqo

Built for the way Morocco messages.

Not a list of buzzwords: here is what happens when a customer writes, sends a voice note, or asks about a product.

01

Understands how Moroccans really message

Customers can write in Darija, Arabic, French or English, including mixed-language messages. Relayqo can understand incoming voice notes and reply in text.

02

WhatsApp first, Instagram when connected

Use the official WhatsApp connection. Instagram DM is available according to your plan and account connection, so the setup is clear before you launch.

03

Product conversations with real catalogue data

For stores, it can use published products, photos, prices, sizes, colours and availability to answer questions and collect an order request.

04

One place for the team to follow through

Customer details and requests appear in the integrated CRM and team inbox. Staff can take over a conversation and track what still needs a human response.

Common questions

What shop owners ask us.

Is Relayqo only for online stores?

No. Service teams can collect enquiries and appointment requests; stores can answer catalogue questions and gather order requests. The setup follows your business.

Can it understand a voice note?

It can understand incoming audio messages and use the relevant business information to reply in text. This is not a promise of generated voice replies.

Does Instagram work too?

Instagram DM support depends on the selected plan and successful account connection. The official WhatsApp channel is the main starting point.

What happens when the bot cannot answer?

The conversation stays visible in the team inbox. Staff can take over, update the approved information, and follow up with the customer.

What do the monthly prices include?

The Services plan is 249 MAD/month and the Commerce plan is 449 MAD/month. We confirm usage allowances, setup needs and any Meta/WhatsApp charges with you before activation; there is no automatic checkout on this site.
