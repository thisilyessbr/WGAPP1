Data deletion

You can ask Relayqo to delete information associated with you or your connected business account.

How to request deletion

  1. Email Ilyes.saber.dev@gmail.com with the subject “Relayqo data deletion request”.
  2. State the WhatsApp number or business account concerned and describe the information you want deleted.
  3. We may ask you to verify control of the account or number before deletion.

What happens next

We will acknowledge the request and, after verification, delete or anonymize the relevant information from active systems within 30 days unless retention is required for security, fraud prevention, disputes, or law. We will confirm completion by email.

Disconnecting Meta

You may also remove Relayqo’s access through your Meta Business settings. Disconnecting access stops future collection but does not replace a deletion request for information already stored.

Instructions en français

Envoyez un e-mail avec l’objet « Relayqo data deletion request », indiquez le numéro WhatsApp ou le compte concerné et les données à supprimer. Après vérification, nous supprimerons ou anonymiserons les données concernées sous 30 jours, sauf obligation de conservation.

تعليمات الحذف بالعربية

أرسل بريدًا بعنوان “Relayqo data deletion request”، واذكر رقم واتساب أو الحساب المعني والبيانات المطلوب حذفها. بعد التحقق، نحذف البيانات أو نخفي هويتها خلال 30 يومًا، إلا إذا كان الاحتفاظ مطلوبًا للأمان أو بموجب القانون.
